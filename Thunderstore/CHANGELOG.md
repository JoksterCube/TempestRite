| **1.0.0** | **Initial Release.** Burn a rite item in the obliterator to perform a rite, for 30 minutes unless said otherwise. **Atmospheric:** the Clear Sky Rite (surtling core: clear skies), Tempest Rite (copper ore: a thunderstorm, wind for sailing, serpent weather), Downpour Rite (2 ooze: rain), Shrouding Rite (3 greydwarf eyes: mist), Aurora Rite (silver necklace: auroras and calm winds). **Celestial:** the Sun Zenith Rite (ruby: day all the time, with day spawns), Midnight Rite (silver ore: night all the time, with night spawns; time, clocks and beds go on as usual under both), Tailwind Rite (dragon egg: wind behind your ship, like Moder's power). **Biome Mastery:** the True Sight Rite (3 refined eitr: no Mistlands mist, off by default), Sootfall Rite (3 smoke puffs: no Ashlands smoke or heat shimmer), Abyssal Surge Rite (2 serpent scales: serpents 3x as often at night and in storms, fish in bigger schools and more big ones). **Domain**, within 100 m of the obliterator: the Packward Rite (9 wolf fangs: hostile creatures flee, 1 hour), Carrion Rite (3 pukeberries: boars, deer and wolves come to it; it and the Packward Rite replace each other), Verdant Rite (9 ancient seeds: plants grow 50% faster), Vat-Boil Rite (2 bile bags: fermenters need half the time, 1 hour), Amber-Mend Rite (50 resin: damaged building pieces are repaired), Sovereign Ward Rite (majestic carapace: built pieces can't be harmed, hostile projectiles crumble, optional Sanctuary Mode), each with its own visual effect, the Unmaking Rite (thunderstone: gear burnt with it gives back 80% of its materials) and the Grounding Rite (flint: ends the Domain rites). **Blessings**, for the players within 10 m of the obliterator, struck by its lightning, until they die: the Crypt Delver Rite (swamp key: never wet, 45 minutes), Glacial Satiety Rite (dragon tear: food fades only to 75%, 90 minutes), Vein-Shatter Rite (hard antler: hits spread through ore veins, which shatter at half health), Carapace Temper Rite (2 mandibles: 60% less gear wear), Grave-Bind Rite (torn spirit: no skill loss on death and a beam over the tombstone, 1 hour), Fortune's Boon Rite (wishbone: most loot, trophies twice as often, trees always give fine wood and seeds), Luminary Rite (5 wisps: a warm light all around you), Abyssal Draw Rite (4 chitin: items fly to you from 12 m). **Incursions:** the Crimson Rite (3 withered bones: raid after raid at the obliterator under a blood moon, and no other raids), Sanctuary Rite (scrap iron: ends the raid going on) and Equilibrium Rite (3 bone fragments: ends every rite at once but the Domain rites and blessings). Each weather rite shows as the biome's own kind of it and never makes a place warmer than its own weather would. Rites in different slots go on together. With several rite items inside, the lowest one decides (the leftmost if several share a row); it is framed in its group's color in the open obliterator, others in gray. Everyone gets the rite and a status with the time left, which stays through death (located rites only near their obliterator). A flap under the open obliterator opens a page listing the rites you know, in tabs by group, where clicking an item puts what the rite needs into the obliterator; a rite is only shown once you have held its item. Each rite can be turned off and has its own duration, item, amount and (for located rites) radius; rites can be limited to admins. What rites do (creatures spawning, fires going out, plants growing, creatures fleeing or coming) is handed to players with the mod nearby, so players without the mod around them get it too. Rites going on are saved with the world, blessings with the character. Required on the server and every player, in the same version; the server can let players without it join (Require Mod On Clients). |
