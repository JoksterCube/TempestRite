| **1.0.1** | **Ragnarök Rite:** anyone who has slain Bonemass (or a stronger boss) now counts Eikthyr and the Elder as slain, so players who missed the early game aren't left out. Three different bosses must be defeated first: if nobody inside has slain Bonemass, the rite calls no boss, says why, and gives its key items back. Shorter on-screen messages. |
| **1.0.0** | **Initial Release.** Place key items in an obliterator and pull the lever: its thunder takes the sacrifice and performs one of **62 rites**, in seven groups shown on a new rites page beside the chest panel: **Atmospheric** (clear skies, storms, rain, mist, auroras), **Celestial** (endless day or night, full wind), **Biome Mastery**, **Domain** (rites that work around the obliterator: faster crops, brewing and smelting, mended buildings, sentry ballistas...), **Blessings** for the players at the obliterator, **Kinship** rites for everyone on the server, and **Reckoning** fights: the Crimson raid, the Ragnarök boss rush and the Hoard-Bane chest fight, each inside a shield bubble with a reward at the end. Each rite has a status that counts down its time, a painted icon and its own look in the world; a compendium lists the rites you have found. Every rite's key item, amount, duration and radius can be changed, or the rite turned off. Needed on the server and every player, in the same version; the server can let only admins perform rites. |
