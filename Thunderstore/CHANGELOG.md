| **1.1.0** | **New: Ley-Survey Rite** (5 coal, Reckoning): for 10 minutes, shows whether a Crimson, Ragnarök or Hoard-Bane Rite could be fought at the obliterator, marking the ring, ground under the sea and everything in the way. A refused fight shows it for 30 seconds. **Reckoning rewards come in treasure chests:** lightning strikes them down around the obliterator, a beam of light over each until opened, split among the biome's own chests and barrels. **Ragnarök rewards reworked:** each boss leaves a chest of resources from its own lands, from common to legendary (even boss summoning items), more per star; no more valuables. New per-boss loot settings, Boss Loot Per Star and Treasure Chests replace Treasure and Treasure Rolls Per Boss. **Hoard-Bane idols:** one idol from every two ages up to yours, instead of two of your current age. Elites and the miniboss stand in slightly different places each fight. **Crimson raids** stay as the game makes them (usual size, no extra stars). Raid Catch-Up (off by default) only uses the game's own stars, so no more starred ghosts. **Fight area renamed the Blood Grounds:** it must be dry ground (floors over water count) with room for creatures, which no longer get stuck in walls, rocks or trees. Bubble settings renamed to Shield and Elite Lair Radius (back to defaults). **Rewards stop growing at 4 players** (new setting: Max Rewarded Players). **Glacial Satiety:** food also lasts 25% longer (new setting: Food Duration Multiplier). **Tundra Shroud and Ragnarök** show only once you have found three different key items they take. **Domain edges** now show on floors built over the ground. **Works with StatusGroups:** rite statuses get their own groups. |
| **1.0.2** | Clearer README: plainer wording, durations listed for every rite, and the True Sight Rite correctly shown as on by default. |
| **1.0.1** | **Ragnarök Rite:** anyone who has slain Bonemass (or a stronger boss) now counts Eikthyr and the Elder as slain, so players who missed the early game aren't left out. Three different bosses must be defeated first: if nobody inside has slain Bonemass, the rite calls no boss, says why, and gives its key items back. Shorter on-screen messages. |
| **1.0.0** | **Initial Release.** Place key items in an obliterator and pull the lever: its thunder takes the sacrifice and performs one of **62 rites**, in seven groups shown on a new rites page beside the chest panel: **Atmospheric** (clear skies, storms, rain, mist, auroras), **Celestial** (endless day or night, full wind), **Biome Mastery**, **Domain** (rites that work around the obliterator: faster crops, brewing and smelting, mended buildings, sentry ballistas...), **Blessings** for the players at the obliterator, **Kinship** rites for everyone on the server, and **Reckoning** fights: the Crimson raid, the Ragnarök boss rush and the Hoard-Bane chest fight, each on Blood Grounds under a shield dome, with a reward at the end. Each rite has a status that counts down its time, a painted icon and its own look in the world; a compendium lists the rites you have found. Every rite's key item, amount, duration and radius can be changed, or the rite turned off. Needed on the server and every player, in the same version; the server can let only admins perform rites. |
